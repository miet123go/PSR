## Struggle for National Democracy
According to Sison, in the struggle of our people for social justice, kailangan nato ug "the struggle for national democracy", which is a necessary stage in 
achieving democractic rights to achieve social emancipation. Kailangan nato na ma-isecure ang freedom sa nation-state in order ang interest only sa masa ang mauna, 
meaning need nato bawalan ang mga laing nasod na manghimasok and pahawaon sila sa atong soil.

"Principal fetters" is what sison refers to what holds us back from achieving national freedom and development. Ang dominasyon sa US sa pilipinas, ug ang semi feudal control ng landlord class sa kanayunan. In order to combat these, sison suggests na kailangan nato mag form ug broad national alliance drawing all classes and strata with an objective interest of overthrowing the alliance of imperialists and landlords.

And then he added na the reason the old communist party failed is because they failed to acknowledge ang ilang tinuod na kalaban which is ang U.S. in a national scale and landlords in a local scale. While some workers daw, marxist or not, demanded philippine independence from U.S., the matter of national liberation daw was obscured by slogans of class struggle between the capitalist class and the working class, instead of the bigger evil.

70% of the pop. in the ph belong to the peasantry, meaning the key to revolutionary victory is the mobilization of the vast masses of peasants. This is called agrarian revolution ug mao ni ang susi para ma emancipate ang mga peasants who were opressed by centuries of feudal and semifeudal exploitation. an agrarian revolution would satisfy the peasants demand for land. "land hunger" ang pwersa na mag tip sa social balance in favor or the revolution.
